## Phase 1: where the category leak came from

The leak came from the **seed data**. The frontend, API filtering, caching and search were not the cause.

- **Seed data (the actual leak):** `CommerceSeeder.cs` assigned categories round-robin (`categories[i % 3]`), which put 14 of the 24 products in the wrong category. "Platform Engineering Handbook", "Cookbook for Weeknights" and "Insulated Water Bottle" were in Electronics, and "Cast Iron Skillet" and "French Press" were in Books. The existing integration test even expected "Mechanical Keyboard" under Home & Kitchen.
- **Parent categories (latent):** the filter matched one exact category slug, so a parent category like Electronics would never include products in subcategories like Audio.
- **Recommendations (latent):** related products were matched on category *name*, so two "Accessories" categories under different parents would share recommendations.

What I changed:
- **Category filter:** `CatalogService.CategoryScope` expands a category to itself plus all its subcategories, using the cached category list. An unknown slug returns nothing instead of every product. Related products now match on category ID.
- **Seeder:** every seed product now names its category explicitly.
- **Existing databases:** migration `20260925123000_FixSeedProductCategories` re-points the 24 seed products by ID. It doesn't change the schema and does nothing on a fresh or non-seeded database; it can be rolled back.
